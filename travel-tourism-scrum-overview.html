Framework

Delivered using SCRUM: 6 sprints, each closing with a Sprint Review and Retrospective. Daily stand-ups track story-point burn-down within every sprint.

Reading the tags

  • SP — Story Points, relative effort sized by the team (Fibonacci: 5 / 8 / 13).
  • Duration — estimated working days to complete the activity.
  • ← Predecessor — the activity(s) that must finish first.

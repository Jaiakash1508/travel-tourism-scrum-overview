# Project Review Presentation and Working Model Video

This file is the content plan for the review deck and a recording script. It is not a .pptx or .mp4 export.

## PowerPoint slide plan

1. **Title** — Roamly: Travel & Tourism Platform; software engineering project review.
2. **Problem** — trip ideas, activities, and budgets are often spread across separate tools.
3. **Goal and users** — help a traveler discover places and shape a simple itinerary; local guides are represented as sample profiles.
4. **Product scope** — destination discovery, search feedback, editable itinerary, cost estimate, sample guide dialog.
5. **Scrum plan** — six sprints, fourteen activities, 115 story points, and 72 days as stated in the supplied overview.
6. **System design** — static HTML/CSS/JavaScript, browser rendering, localStorage; refer to UML-Diagrams.md.
7. **Working model** — show destination cards, add a destination, add a custom activity, and see the estimated total change.
8. **More interactions** — remove an item, reload to demonstrate browser persistence, and open a guide profile.
9. **Limitations** — sample data only; no live search, travel API, account, backend, collaboration, booking, or payment.
10. **Conclusion and next steps** — prototype validates the front-end flow; propose secure accounts, shared trips, real data sources, accessibility/usability evaluation.

## Suggested visual treatment
Use a clean travel editorial style: warm off-white backgrounds, forest green accents, one destination photograph per section, brief captions, and large readable labels. Keep the prototype limitations visible on the demo slide.

## Working model video storyboard (about 90 seconds)

- **0:00–0:08 — Title:** show the Roamly homepage and introduce the travel planning concept.
- **0:08–0:20 — Discover:** scroll through three illustrative destination cards and point out category and sample daily estimate.
- **0:20–0:38 — Add an idea:** add a destination to the itinerary; show activity count and budget changing.
- **0:38–0:55 — Custom activity:** enter “Sunset harbor walk” and a sample cost, add it, then explain the updated total.
- **0:55–1:08 — Edit:** remove an activity and show totals recalculate; reload to show this browser retains the itinerary.
- **1:08–1:20 — Local guide concept:** open a sample guide profile and clarify that this is illustrative only.
- **1:20–1:30 — Close:** summarize the prototype and state that live search, availability, bookings, and payments are not connected.

## Recording notes
Record the browser at 1080p with the pointer visible, use a fresh demo itinerary, avoid exposing unrelated browser tabs or personal information, and narrate the sample-data limitations. The video itself still needs to be recorded/exported as MP4.
